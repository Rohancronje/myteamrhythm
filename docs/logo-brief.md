# Logo Design Brief — "Rhythm"

## 1. The product in one line
**Rhythm** is a volunteer connection platform for churches. It helps ministry
coaches stay personally connected to their volunteers — knowing who to reach out to,
remembering birthdays, and sending a word of encouragement — so no one on the team
runs on empty. Tagline in use: *"Planning Center tells you who's scheduled; Rhythm
tells you who's running on empty."*

- **Product name / wordmark:** Rhythm
- **Domain:** myteamrhythm.online
- **First customer:** NS Family Services (a City Impact church campus, New Zealand)
- **Category:** modern SaaS / people-care tool (not a "churchy" clip-art brand)

## 2. Who it's for
Church "coaches" and ministry leaders — warm, relational people, often volunteers
themselves, aged ~25–55. They open Rhythm on a phone between services and on a laptop
during the week. The brand should feel **caring, calm, and trustworthy**, with a
quietly modern, premium edge — think a wellbeing app crossed with a good CRM, not a
Sunday-school poster.

## 3. Brand personality
- **Human & warm** — it's about people, not data.
- **Steady & dependable** — "rhythm" = a healthy, sustainable cadence of care.
- **Uplifting & hopeful** — encouragement, faithfulness, promise.
- **Modern & clean** — confident SaaS, not busy or ornate.
- Faith-informed but **inclusive and understated** — no overt religious clichés
  (no doves, crosses, praying hands, fish). Symbolism should be subtle enough to live
  comfortably on any church's dashboard.

## 4. Concept territory (ideas, not prescriptions)
The name "Rhythm" invites motion, cadence, connection and heartbeat. Explore:
- **Cadence / pulse** — a gentle waveform, heartbeat line, or evenly-spaced beats
  that could double as people or dots.
- **Connection** — two elements linked, overlapping, or in orbit (mirrors the app's
  "connect" icon: two circles joined by a line).
- **People in a healthy loop** — a circle/ring suggesting an ongoing care cycle
  (the app already uses weekly "dot" rhythms and rings).
- **A quiet nod to growth or steadiness** — without leaning on literal church iconography.

We're open to a **wordmark-led** solution, a **standalone symbol**, or (ideally) a
**wordmark + a mark that also works as a standalone app icon**.

## 5. Look & feel (align with the live product)
The app is a **dark, glassy, premium UI** with a signature gradient. The logo should
feel native to it, but also work in a single flat colour.

- **Signature gradient:** violet → pink, `#8B6CFF → #FF5C8A` (used for the brand
  wordmark and primary actions).
- **Accents:** mint `#38DD9B`, sky blue `#5CC2FF`.
- **Dark canvas:** near-black plum `#0A0912` / deep surfaces around `#141225`.
- **Light text:** off-white `#F4F2FF`.
- Current wordmark is set in a bold, friendly geometric-humanist sans with a subtle
  gradient fill. Feel free to propose a better-crafted custom wordmark.

## 6. Deliverables
1. **Primary logo** (wordmark + symbol) — full colour on dark, plus a variant for light backgrounds.
2. **Standalone symbol / app icon** — square, legible at 32×32 and as a favicon (16×16).
3. **Monochrome versions** — single-colour (white, black) for stamps, invoices, emails.
4. **Horizontal and stacked lock-ups**, with defined clear-space and minimum sizes.
5. Source files: **SVG (primary)** + layered vector source (AI/Figma), plus exported
   PNGs at common sizes. A short one-page usage sheet (colours, spacing, don'ts).

## 7. Usage contexts
Top-left of a desktop sidebar (small), mobile app header, favicon/browser tab,
transactional emails (e.g. coach invites), and social/OG preview cards. It must stay
crisp and recognisable when tiny and in one colour.

## 8. Do / Don't
- **Do** keep it simple, scalable, and confident; make the symbol work without the word.
- **Do** let it feel warm and human — soft geometry, gentle motion.
- **Don't** use literal religious icons (cross, dove, fish, hands), musical clefs/notes
  taken too literally, generic "swoosh + circle" startup clichés, or heavy drop-shadows.
- **Don't** rely on the gradient to carry the whole idea — it must survive as one flat colour.

## 9. Practical
- **Formats:** vector-first, delivered as described in §6.
- **Timeline / budget:** [to be filled in by client].
- **Approval:** present 2–3 distinct directions first (concept sketches), then refine one.

## 10. Reference points to react to
Warm-but-modern wellbeing/CRM brands (calm palettes, rounded humanist type, a single
memorable mark). The overall feeling target: *"a trusted friend who helps me care for
my people well."*
